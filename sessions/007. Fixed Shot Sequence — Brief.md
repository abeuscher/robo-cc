## Goal

Replace the Attacker's free choice of weapon on every shot with a fixed, scripted sequence:
Catapult, Catapult, Ballista, in that order, three shots total instead of six. The Attacker no
longer picks a weapon at all -- the server dictates it per shot, and the client just adapts its
aiming/HUD to whichever weapon is currently mandated.

  - `AttackInput` holds a local `state.weapon` the player freely toggles with `F` at any point

    choice and becomes a server-derived fact of "which shot number this is."

## Plan

### Phase 1 — `Config`

- Replace the bare `ShotsPerTurn = 6` with a `ShotSequence = { "Catapult", "Catapult",
  "Ballista" }` array; `ShotsPerTurn` becomes `#ShotSequence` so the two can never drift apart,
  the same way `GridSize` now derives from `BlockSize` (session 005).

### Phase 2 — `MatchController` — the current shot's weapon

- Add a way to read "which weapon is the shot about to be fired" from `Config.ShotSequence` and
  `state.shotsRemaining` (the shot index counts up as `shotsRemaining` counts down). Broadcast
  it as a new `currentWeapon` field on the existing `StateChanged` payload, `nil` outside
  ATTACK.

### Phase 3 — `WeaponManager` — server dictates the weapon

- `onFireWeapon` stops reading `payload.weapon` (and validating it) entirely; it reads the
  mandated weapon from `MatchController` at the top of the handler -- before `consumeShot()`
  runs, since that advances the shot index -- and fires that, ignoring anything the client might
  send.

### Phase 4 — `Types`

- `ShotPayload` drops the `weapon` field (the client no longer sends one).
- `StatePayload` gains `currentWeapon: Weapon?`.

### Phase 5 — Client (`AttackInput`, `Hud`)

- `AttackInput` drops the `F` key handling entirely. `state.weapon` becomes a mirror of
  `StateChanged.currentWeapon` instead of free-toggled local state -- updated (and pushed to
  `WeaponView`/the HUD readout) both on activation and whenever it changes mid-turn, i.e.
  between shot 2 (Catapult) and shot 3 (Ballista), not just on the ATTACK-phase transition.
- `Hud`'s attacker hint line drops "F weapon."

### Phase 6 — Playtest

Two-player Studio playtest: the Attacker gets exactly 3 shots, weapon 1 and 2 are Catapult with
no way to switch to Ballista, weapon 3 is Ballista with no way to switch back, and the HUD/prop
model correctly reflect whichever weapon is currently mandated at every point in the turn.

## Out of scope

  two-click launch meter) — unchanged, just which weapon is available when.
- Further tuning of the sequence, shot count, or weapon numbers beyond expressing this
  session's specific ask — stays Pass 9, "Tune, then launch."
- BUILD-phase / piece-randomization work — Pass 8, untouched here.
