# Session 015 — Hidden Flag & Rainbow Wall — Brief

**Roadmap section:** Pass 15 — Hidden Flag & Rainbow Wall (`roadmap.md`)
**Branch:** session-015

## Goal

Replace the Princess with a Flag that's hidden from the Attacker until struck, scored only on a
direct projectile hit, and repositionable by the Builder once the wall is confirmed. Separately,
give every placed wall piece a distinct color from a fixed pastel rainbow palette. Two independent
changes, bundled because both touch how the wall and its target are presented.

## Starting state

- The Princess (`Arena.luau`) is a real unanchored body armed at ATTACK start
  (`PrincessMonitor.arm()`), killed by a continuous `Heartbeat` tilt check against
  `Config.PrincessTopplingAngle` (session 009), scoring the Attacker a flat `PrincessKillPoints`
  (15, session 012) on top of the Builder's `PointsPerSurvivingBlock` (1 per surviving wall body,
  unaffected by this session).
- Every wall body is a single flat grey (`BLOCK_COLOR` in `BlockManager.luau`), regardless of
  which of the round's `Config.PieceCount` (10) placements it came from.
- Agreed going into this session (from the session 014 design conversation, not yet built):
  - The Flag doesn't topple; it's hit or it isn't.
  - Hidden from the Attacker until struck by *either* a projectile or a falling wall piece — either
    one reveals it for the rest of the round.
  - Only a **projectile** hit scores the Attacker's bonus (raising it 15 -> 20); a wall-collapse
    hit reveals the Flag but scores nothing. This gives the Attacker a real, separate mechanism
    (break the wall onto it) for forcing the reveal, distinct from what actually scores.
  - Concealment is real, not a disguise: `BasePart.LocalTransparencyModifier` hides the Flag on
    the Attacker's client only (Builder's view and the underlying physics are both unaffected);
    paired with excluding the Flag from the Attacker's own client-side aim-assist raycasts while
    hidden, so the crosshair and dashed trace can't leak its position. A real shot can still
    physically connect either way — the concealment only defeats the assist tools, not the
    physics.
  - The Builder can reposition the Flag once, after the wall is confirmed, before ATTACK begins.
  - The wall's rainbow palette is generated, not hand-picked: evenly spaced hues at a fixed pastel
    saturation/value via `Color3.fromHSV`, sized to `Config.PieceCount` so a later retune of piece
    count doesn't leave the palette short or bunched. Colors apply per placement, not per body — a
    piece that fractures into several independent bodies (session 008) keeps them all one color.
- Rojo pipeline: known-good as of session 014 (confirm with a build after the first edit anyway,
  per process rules).

## Plan

### Phase 1 — Config and shared types

Add the Flag's geometry and point constants (replacing the Princess's own); remove
`PrincessTopplingAngle`. Add the palette formula/constant(s). Update `Types.RoundResult.reason` for
the new outcome distinction (projectile hit vs. wall-collapse hit vs. survived).

### Phase 2 — Server: Flag hit detection and scoring

Rewrite `PrincessMonitor` (renamed) around `Touched`, distinguishing a projectile from a falling
wall piece from anything else. Wire `MatchController`'s scoring branch: projectile hit -> Builder's
survivor count still applies, Attacker gets the raised bonus; wall-collapse hit -> reveal only, no
Attacker bonus; neither -> Flag survives, same as today. `WeaponManager` tags projectiles for
reliable identification if `BlockId`-style attributes don't already cover it.

### Phase 3 — Concealment

`LocalTransparencyModifier` toggle for the Attacker's client, plus the `AttackInput` raycast
exclusion while hidden. Reveal needs to propagate to the Attacker's client the instant it happens
(mid-turn, same category of "broadcast it on every relevant state change" lesson sessions 007/008
already established for the mandated weapon and the held shape).

### Phase 4 — Flag relocation

Builder repositions the Flag once, after `ConfirmBuild`, before ATTACK unanchors things. Exact
mechanism is this session's own Open Gate decision (see below) — implement whichever is chosen.

### Phase 5 — Rainbow wall

`BlockManager` assigns each placement (not each body) a color from the generated palette, indexed
by placement order.

### Phase 6 — Playtest and verify

Two-player playtest covering the new target end to end: wall goes up in rainbow colors, Flag stays
hidden through early shots, a wall-collapse hit reveals it without scoring, a direct projectile hit
reveals it and scores the raised bonus, and the round/match loop (including sudden death, from
session 014) still resolves correctly around the new outcome.

## Open questions

Settle these at the Open Gate:

- **Flag relocation mechanism.** A single click to relocate before finalizing (cheapest — reuses
  the existing grid-click pattern `BuildInput` already has) vs. a freer drag within a bounded zone.
  Needs an answer before Phase 4.
- **Movement axis.** Guard and Shield currently sit at fixed distances directly in front of the
  Princess on one central axis (`GuardDistance`/`ShieldDistance`, both derived from
  `PrincessDistance`). If the Flag can only move along that same axis (closer/farther, not
  sideways), Guard/Shield need no changes at all. If it can move off-axis, Guard/Shield either need
  to start tracking it or the Flag's movement needs bounding so it can't just sidestep them
  entirely — decide which before Phase 4.
- **Reveal permanence.** Assumed default: once revealed (by either cause), the Flag stays visible
  and targetable for the rest of the round — no re-hiding. Confirm, since it's cheap to change now
  and awkward once `FlagMonitor` is built around it.
- **Visual feedback on reveal.** Beyond simply becoming visible, does anything else mark the
  moment (a color change, a brief highlight)? Not required, but worth a decision before Phase 3
  rather than an afterthought.

  saw. Then stop — do not start the close until the user says so.
