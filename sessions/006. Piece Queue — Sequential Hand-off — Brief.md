## Goal

Replace the BUILD-phase pile (12 pre-spawned pieces sitting in a stack, freely liftable and
re-placeable in any order) with a sequential hand-off: the Builder holds exactly one piece at
a time and must place it before the next one is issued. No physical pile geometry sits in the
trench anymore -- the ghost preview is the only thing the Builder sees of a piece before it's
placed. The piece shape itself is unchanged (still the session 005 L-piece, still 12 of them)
-- this session is purely the interaction-model swap. Session 007 swaps the fixed shape for a
per-piece randomly generated one; keeping that out of this session is what makes each half
independently testable.

## Starting state

- Session 005 (L-piece pilot, generalized shape/rotation pipeline) is implemented and
  playtested green, plus a follow-up tweak (blocks are now 1.25-stud cubes, not flat bricks).
  `GridUtil`'s shape/rotation math and `BuildInput`'s per-cell ghost pool are already fully
  generic — this session's changes lean on both without modifying either.
- The current model, precisely, before this session touches it:
  - `BlockManager.spawnAll()` pre-spawns all 12 L-pieces as anchored `UnionOperation`s into a
    physical pile at `Config.BlockTypes[1].PileOrigin`, stacked upward.
  - `BuildInput` requires a click on a pile piece (a real raycast hit) to "lift" it into
    `held`; only then does the ghost appear. Clicking a placed wall piece (empty-handed) sends
    it back to the pile (`PickupBlock` -> `BlockManager.returnToPile`), and the Builder can do
    this in any order, any number of times, before `ConfirmBuild`.
  - `Config`'s `PILE_MARGIN`/`PILE_GAP`/`PILE_Z` and the per-type `PileOrigin` math size
    `PlatformSize` to fit the pile alongside the build zone. `Arena.build()` draws a marker
    part per type at its `PileOrigin`.
  - None of this survives removing the pile unchanged — spawning shifts from "all up front"
    to "on demand as each piece is issued," and the client's lift-from-pile gesture disappears
    entirely since there's nothing physical to click before placement.
- Rojo pipeline: known-good (`rojo build` after every change, no lint/type tooling installed).

## Plan

### Phase 1 — `Config` / `Arena` cleanup

- Remove the pile-spacing constants and per-type `PileOrigin` math; `PlatformSize` no longer
  needs to reserve room for a pile. Keep `BlockTypes[1].Count = 12` unchanged for this pass —
  the piece count only becomes meaningful to revisit once session 007 changes what a piece is.
- `Arena.build()` drops its per-type pile-marker loop.

### Phase 2 — `BlockManager` — issue on demand

- Replace "pre-spawn all 12 into a pile" with a queue: track how many of the round's pieces
  have been issued/placed. A piece's physical `UnionOperation` is only built at the moment it's
  placed (`moveToWall` becomes "build + place" instead of "reposition a pre-existing part") --
  there's no physical part to manage before that, so no pile, no hidden-while-held part on the
  client.
- `releaseAll()` still unanchors whatever's actually on the wall at ATTACK start; unplaced
  queue remainder just never got instantiated, so there's nothing to destroy for them.

### Phase 3 — `BuildManager` — sequential validation

- Track queue position per round (reset on `onRoundReset`, same pattern as `resetOccupancy`).
- `PlaceBlock` validates against "the current issued piece," not an arbitrary previously-spawned
  `blockId`. On acceptance, advance the queue and let the client know what's next (or that the
  queue is empty).
- Resolve the two open questions below before this phase is implemented, since they change
  what `PlaceBlock`/`PickupBlock`/`ConfirmBuild` actually validate.

### Phase 4 — Client — auto-hold, no pile click

- `BuildInput` no longer needs a raycast-click to lift a piece; as soon as BUILD starts (or the
  previous piece is placed), the next piece is simply held and the ghost is live. The
  hover/rotate/place flow (ghost cubes, `R`, click-to-place) is otherwise unchanged.
- `Hud`'s blocks-remaining line changes from a pile count to a queue position ("Piece 4 of 12"
  or similar).

### Phase 5 — Playtest

Two-player Studio playtest: no pile geometry visible, the Builder always has exactly one piece
in hand, placing it correctly issues the next one, and the round still reaches `ConfirmBuild` /
ATTACK correctly with the wall built entirely through the sequential flow.

## Open questions

Settle these at session start — they change what Phase 3's remotes validate, not just how:

- **Undo / rearrange.** Spec currently says the Builder "can pick up and re-place before
  confirming." Does that survive at all in the sequential model — e.g., can the Builder click
  an already-placed piece to pull it back into their hand (undoing that placement and
  re-queuing it as current), or is a placement final the moment it lands? Recommend keeping a
  limited undo (click the most recently placed piece only) rather than none, since losing all
  ability to correct a misplaced piece is a real usability regression from today -- but this is
  the user's call, not mine to assume.
- **Early confirm.** Can the Builder press Confirm Wall before the queue is exhausted (ending
  BUILD with fewer than 12 pieces placed), or does BUILD only end once every piece is placed
  (with Confirm Wall removed or simply redundant at that point)?
