# Session 002 — ATTACK phase & physics — Log

**Date:** 2026-09-04
**Branch:** session-002 (worked on local branch `session-002/1`, matching session 001's drift)
**Roadmap section:** Pass 2 — ATTACK phase + physics

## What was built

Open questions from the brief were settled at the Open Gate: (1) un-placed pile blocks are
removed outright in `releaseAll()`, (2) a separate `BallistaImpact` constant (starting equal to
`CatapultImpact`), (3) muzzle origin is a fixed point (attacker-side gap edge, `x=0`) plus a
`MuzzleHeight` constant, not tied to the avatar or camera.

### Phase 1 — Shared plumbing + wall release

- `Remotes.luau` — added `FireWeapon`.
- `Config.luau` — added `MuzzleHeight`, `BallistaImpact`.
- `Types.luau` — added `shotsRemaining: number` to `StatePayload`.
- `BlockManager.releaseAll()` — placed blocks: unanchor then `SetNetworkOwner(nil)`; un-placed
  pile blocks: destroyed.

### Phase 2 — `WeaponManager.luau` (server, new)

- Handles `FireWeapon`: validates Attacker + `phase == "ATTACK"` + a shot available via
  `MatchController.consumeShot()`, then spawns a projectile and steps it on `Heartbeat`.
- Projectile is an **anchored**, manually-integrated Part (deviation from the brief's
  unanchored/`SetNetworkOwner` sketch — noted and accepted at the time: same
  server-authoritative raycast hits, no integrator to fight Roblox's own physics for).
- Ballista and catapult both take their launch speed/force through the same `force` payload
  field, clamped server-side to their own Config range.
- Knockback: `ApplyImpulse(dir * impact * block.AssemblyMass)` — mass-scaled, a deviation from
  the roadmap's literal `dir * Impact` (which would have been too weak, ~2.4 studs/s, to topple
  anything at Standard block mass).

### Phase 3 — Shot loop (server)

- `MatchController` — `confirmBuild()` now releases the wall, sets `shotsRemaining =
  Config.ShotsPerTurn`, and broadcasts. `consumeShot()` decrements and rebroadcasts; holds at 0.
  `isAttacker()` added alongside the existing `isBuilder()`.
- `init.server.luau` — starts `WeaponManager`.

### Phase 4 — `AttackInput.luau` (client, new) and Phase 5 — HUD

- Built, then rebuilt repeatedly across the playtest rounds below — see Post-gate work. The
  brief's original plan (mouse aim, wheel force, Q/E angle, F weapon, click fire, a straight
  aim-preview beam) did not survive contact with playtesting; its final shape is described
  there instead of here.
- `Hud.luau` — shots remaining, weapon + readout during ATTACK; BUILD counters/hint hidden;
  moved to the bottom-right corner (mid-session, see below).

## Playtest result

Playtested by the user with two local Studio clients (finding the 2-player test flow in the
newer "Next Gen" Studio UI took some back-and-forth — the old Test-tab "Clients and Servers"
player-count box doesn't exist in that theme; the working path was the Play-button dropdown's
"Server & Clients" entry). Iterated through many rounds of fixes after each round of feedback:

1. **First pass** — a stray `SpawnLocation` (from the baseplate template) sat in the middle of
   the trench; the ballista hit the wall's second row instead of the base; the catapult
   couldn't be seen firing; the fixed camera didn't help visibility.
   - Fixed: `Arena.build()` now strips any foreign `SpawnLocation` and adds its own hidden one;
     `MuzzleHeight` lowered to bottom-row height and the ballista's aim flattened to horizontal
     only (it was taking full vertical mouse aim, contrary to spec's "flat trajectory"); a side
     camera (`AttackCamera`, new) was added for the Attacker; `CatapultMaxForce` retuned down so
     shots land in the arena; the projectile got a trail, a `PointLight`, and a size bump.
2. **Crosshair + projectile visibility** — replaced the full-length trajectory beam with a
   world-space crosshair (BillboardGui + Frames) at the traced impact point, plus a light
   dashed line along the path. Crosshair turns green when the predicted impact is a wall block.
3. **Full targeting/camera/selection redesign requested** — trench sizing, fixed weapon
   emplacements with turn-start selection, and a three-mode camera (top-down / first-person /
   45°-side). Scoped as too large to fold into this session (arena/physics-model change,
   multi-module camera system); the small, low-risk pieces (dashed line, green crosshair, HUD
   reposition) were done here, the rest deferred to session 003 (brief drafted at close).
4. **Launch meter** — replaced the wheel-driven catapult force with a two-click golf-swing
   meter (right-edge bar, target tick, `TweenService`-driven fill). Iterated three times on
   user feedback: slowed 4x, switched from `Elastic` to `Linear` easing with the loop removed
   (one up-and-down pass, then stop; press again for a fresh attempt), and the 3D crosshair/
   dashed line now hide entirely during the swing instead of live-tracking it.
5. **Ballista parity + camera swap** — gave the ballista the same two-click meter (its muzzle
   speed now scales `BallistaMinVelocity..BallistaMuzzleVelocity` with the meter fill; target
   tick pins to the top, since more power always hits harder rather than farther). Swapped the
   fixed side camera for a player-controlled view.
6. **Targeting precision** — diagnosed and fixed two real bugs: the ballista's crosshair used a
   stepped-integrator raycast (~10 studs/step at muzzle speed) that made it feel like it was
   snapping to a grid on the wall; replaced with a single continuous raycast (the flight is
   near-flat, so this is exact, not an approximation). Separately, the catapult's own aim ray
   was being stopped by the wall itself, capping how far "over the wall" the player could aim
   even though the real fired arc was never actually blocked — fixed by excluding the block
   wall from the catapult's aim raycast specifically (matches spec: "ignores wall height if
   aimed well"), while the real simulated arc (used for the crosshair/hit-color and the actual
   shot) still correctly raycasts against blocks.
7. **Camera zoom** — the player-controlled camera landed on `LockFirstPerson` after step 5;
   swapped for a locked third-person distance (`CameraMinZoomDistance == CameraMaxZoomDistance
   == 8` studs) per feedback that true first-person was too tight.

User's final assessment: "in good shape for the first pass." No open bugs at close; remaining
work is the deferred scope in session 003.

## Config changes

- `MuzzleHeight` — new, `2` at first pass → retuned to `0.75` (bottom-row height) after the
  ballista-hits-row-2 bug.
- `BallistaImpact` — new, `60` (starts equal to `CatapultImpact`, per the brief's
  recommendation; untested against `CatapultImpact`'s own divergence — both are Pass 4 tuning
  surface).
- `BallistaMinVelocity` — new, `190`, added late for the ballista's launch-meter parity;
  `BallistaMuzzleVelocity` (`320`, unchanged) is the ceiling.
- `CatapultMaxForce` — `220` → `150`, retuned so a mid-meter shot lands inside the arena at
  this Princess distance rather than overshooting badly.
- `CatapultMinForce` (`80`), `CatapultMinAngle`/`CatapultMaxAngle` (`20`/`70`),
  `BallistaMuzzleVelocity` (`320`), `BallistaGravityScale` (`0.05`), `CatapultImpact` (`60`),
  `ShotsPerTurn` (`6`) — unchanged from session 001.
