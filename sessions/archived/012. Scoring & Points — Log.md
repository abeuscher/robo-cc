# Session 012 — Scoring & Points — Log

**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Branch:** session-012
**Roadmap section:** Pass 12 — Scoring & Points

## What was built

### Phase 1 — `Config` and the scoring data shape

- Added `PointsPerSurvivingBlock` (1), `PrincessKillPoints` (15 — derived as
  `round(PieceCount * PieceMaxCells * PointsPerSurvivingBlock / 4)`, i.e. ~1/4 of the theoretical
  maximum wall score under worst-case fragmentation), and `BlockTopplingAngle` (60 degrees — a
  placed body's own orientation-drift threshold, mirroring `PrincessTopplingAngle`'s mechanism).
- Reshaped `Types.RoundResult` from `{ winner: Role, reason }` to
  `{ reason, piecesSurvived, piecesTotal, builderPoints, attackerPoints, winner: Role? }` (`winner`
  now nilable — a tied round has none). `Types.Score`'s comment updated from "rounds won" to
  "cumulative points."

### Phase 2 — Server: measure and score

- `BlockManager` gained `totalCount()` (every body placed this round) and `survivingCount()`
  (bodies still within `BlockTopplingAngle` of how they were placed). Each body's placement
  `CFrame` is stashed as a `PlacedCFrame` attribute at placement time; `survivingCount()` compares
  it to the body's current `CFrame` via `ToObjectSpace(...):ToAxisAngle()` — body-level, not by
  original Builder placement, since one placement can already split into several independent
  bodies (session 008) and `BlockManager` already tracked them flatly.
- `MatchController.finishRound()` rewritten around points: `builderPoints = piecesSurvived *
  PointsPerSurvivingBlock`, `attackerPoints = PrincessKillPoints` if the Princess toppled else 0,
  both added into `state.score` (still keyed by `UserId`, semantics shifted to cumulative points).
  A per-round `winner` is now whichever side scored higher that round (nil on a tie) — display
  only; match-level `finishMatch()` needed no change since it already just compared `state.score`
  entries.

### Phase 3 — Client

- `ResultBanner`'s round-result banner now shows the full breakdown (e.g. "Builder 8 pts (6/10
  pieces survived) · Attacker 15 pts (knocked off her feet)") and handles a tied round explicitly;
  widened the banner and wrapped the subtitle to fit. Match-result and `Hud`'s running score both
  relabeled from bare numbers to "N pts" for clarity now that they're points, not rounds won.

### Open Gate decisions

- **Piece vs. body:** body-level, per the brief's own steer — matches `BlockManager`'s existing
  flat tracking, no new grouping needed.
- **What "survived" means:** settled on an orientation check (angle drift from placement,
  `BlockTopplingAngle`), not a spatial "off the platform" check. Reading `Arena.luau` during the
  reading list found the arena is one continuous flat `Ground` part fully enclosed by boundary
  walls on all four sides, with no pit — a wall piece cannot physically leave the platform under
  the current geometry, so a literal spatial edge check would have almost nothing to trigger on.
  Confirmed at the gate rather than assumed.

### Mid-session tuning (playtest feedback, not itself Pass 12 scope)

- `Config.CatapultProjectileMass` raised 35 → 43.75 (+25%) — the catapult still wasn't knocking
  walls down convincingly even after session 011's angle retune. Chosen over a force/velocity
  change since mass doesn't affect trajectory under Roblox's gravity (same principle already
  noted for the ballista), so aim/reach stayed exactly as tuned.
- `AttackInput`'s `METER_LEG_TIME` cut 3.4s → 2.4s (30% faster launch meter).
- `Config.ShotSequence` grown from 3 shots (`Catapult, Catapult, Ballista`) to 5
  (`Catapult, Catapult, Catapult, Ballista, Ballista`) after the mass/meter retune still wasn't
  enough on its own. `ShotsPerTurn` derives from the sequence length, so nothing else needed
  updating.

## Playtest result

Two-player Studio playtest run by the user across several iterations. The first pass surfaced that
the catapult wasn't knocking walls down enough to produce a meaningful spread of piece-survival
scores; that fed the mass and meter-speed retunes. A follow-up pass still found it "a bit
difficult," which led to extending the shot sequence from 3 to 5 shots. After that change, the
user's assessment was "Okay looking good."

Not independently itemized this session: the brief's Phase 4 called for checking a spread of
specific outcomes (wall mostly intact, wall mostly destroyed, early Princess kill, late Princess
kill) individually. The playtest conversation confirmed the points system reads correctly in
general ("I see the difference") and that difficulty felt right after the shot-sequence extension,
but didn't call out each outcome case by name. Worth a closer look if scoring specifics come up
again.
